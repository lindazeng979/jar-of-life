    The lesson

    The jar is your time and energy, and it doesn't get any bigger. What you put in first decides what fits.

    What it teaches

    Order matters
    If you fill the jar with sand or pebbles first, there's no room left for the big rocks.
    Prioritize first
    If you spend your time and energy on small tasks and distractions first, you'll never get to the things that matter. Put the big rocks in first, let the pebbles fill the spaces between, and let the sand settle around them.
    Leave room for fun
    Once the important things are in, there's still room for a beer or a slow evening with friends. A full life has space for joy too.
