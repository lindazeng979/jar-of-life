# Jar of Life 🫙

Your life is a jar. Put the big rocks in first, let the pebbles fill the gaps, let the sand settle, and leave room for a beer (or coffee, or boba) with a friend. 

Jar of Life is a little interactive reminder to prioritize what matters most. It turns your daily to-do list into a jar you can fill with the things that make up your life.

**Build your own daily priority jar:** [lindazeng979.github.io/jar-of-life/](https://lindazeng979.github.io/jar-of-life/).

> Inspired by this [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n5rr6sHOtUE) by Stephen R. Covey's big rocks analogy.

![Jar of Life Preview](assets/jar-preview.png)

## Features
- Type an activity (e.g. "dance") and drop it into the jar as a Big Rock, Pebble, Sand or Beer. The pieces fall with real physics.
- Editable suggestion lists for each category.
- Tap any piece (in the jar or in the list) to rename it, move it to another category, or delete it (with undo). Drag pieces around.
- **Sort it out**: a short quiz using Skip It / Delegation / Restoration tests, with general or college-student wording.
- **Brain dump**: write everything down, circle your big rocks, cross out the sand, then split the rest.
- **Pour in order** / **Try the wrong order** show why order matters.
- Custom colors and presets, an adjustable jar size, and a choice of drink (or type your own).
- Your jar is saved only in your own browser. There is no server and nothing is uploaded.
- "Copy a link to this jar" packs your jar into a link (after the `#`, which is never sent to a server), so you can open it on another device. Export/import works too.

## Run locally
Open `index.html`, or run `python3 -m http.server` in this folder and visit http://localhost:8000.

Built with plain HTML/CSS/JS and [Matter.js](https://brm.io/matter-js/) for physics.
